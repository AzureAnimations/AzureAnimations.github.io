Lab guides

Labs tell you what to type.
They rarely show you what you’re building.

A hands-on lab is a wall of numbered instructions. You follow them, they work — and you still finish without a clear picture of what you made, or why. These guides add that picture: one screen per idea, one panel per task.

1What am I building?

The finished thing is on screen from the start, greyed out. Every task lights up one more piece of it.

2What will I be able to do?

Each task closes with the capability it earns you — not a summary of the steps you just typed.

3How far am I?

Every task is a button. You can see which are core, which are optional, and jump straight to the one you care about.
