TEAMS DEMO CHANNEL - READY-TO-PASTE MESSAGES
Outlook + Teams: Wattle Road and Riverside upgrade (fictional service-coordination scenario)
Instructor use only. All names and details are made up and follow the eight practice emails. Nothing new is decided here.

=====================================================================
SET UP (about 10 minutes, no admin rights needed)
=====================================================================
1. In Teams, use a team you own (or make a private one: Teams > Join or create a team > Create a team > From scratch > Private).
2. Add a channel: next to the team name click "..." > Add channel. Name it:  Wattle Road Upgrade
3. Post the 5 STARTER messages below, one at a time. Copy one block, click "Start a post" in the channel, paste, press Send.
4. Under a starter, click Reply and post that thread's REPLY messages in order, one at a time.
5. Do this on the day of the workshop (or the day before). Demo 3 asks Copilot about "the past seven days", so the messages must be recent.

Good to know
- Every message appears as sent by YOU, at the real time. The first line of each block (name and role) tells the story.
- Paste one block per message. Pasting several blocks at once creates a single long message.
- Copilot in Teams needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. If Copilot is missing in the channel, check the licence first.
- Use only this channel for Demo 3. Do not post real project or resident information here.
