MERIDIAN RETAIL - ENGAGEMENT TEAM (mock channel transcript for Session 5: Copilot in Teams)

All Meridian names, entities, amounts, transactions, emails, chats and findings are fictional training data.
Copilot proposes; the team verifies. This is a training exercise, not audit or tax advice.

HOW TO USE: a facilitator can post these messages into a TRAINING chat or channel (never a real client conversation) so learners can try Copilot on them.
Day 1 to Day 3 are relative days. Message numbers (#1 to #25) are added for this training so that summaries can cite them. People without a name are role labels only.
