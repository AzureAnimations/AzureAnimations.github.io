TEAMS DEMO CHANNEL - READY-TO-PASTE MESSAGES
Outlook + Teams: Run My Day (Hospitality, fictional hotel: Riverside Grand)
Trainer use only. All names, rooms and numbers are made up.

=====================================================================
SET UP (about 10 minutes, no admin rights needed)
=====================================================================
1. In Teams, go to a team you own (or make one: Teams > Join or create a team > Create a team > From scratch > Private).
2. Add a channel: next to the team name click "..." > Add channel. Name it:  Conference Day
3. Post the 4 STARTER messages below, one at a time. Copy one block, click "Start a post" in the channel, paste, press Send.
4. Under each starter, click Reply and post that thread's REPLY messages in order, one at a time.
5. Optional: pin the channel to your favourites, and add a thumbs-up to a couple of messages.

Good to know
- Every message appears as sent by YOU, at the real time. The first line of each block (name and time) tells the story.
- Paste one block per message. Pasting several blocks at once creates a single long message.
- "@Coordinator" is plain text. If you want a real mention, type @ and pick a person.
- Copilot in Teams needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. If "Summarise" is missing, check the licence first.
- This was written from the 15 practice emails. It adds no new facts.

DEMO PROMPTS (use Copilot in the channel)
=====================================================================

DEMO 1 - CATCH UP
Summarise what I missed in this channel today. Show important updates, decisions, unresolved issues, unanswered questions, and actions with owners. Include references to the relevant messages.

DEMO 2 - SUMMARISE BY TOPIC
Organise today's channel discussion by topic: conference hall facilities, catering, audio and visual equipment, and guest communication. Under each topic, show the latest status, decisions made, open questions, and next action.

DEMO 3 - FIND MESSAGES REQUIRING ATTENTION
Which messages in this channel require a response or action? Prioritise them based on operational impact and deadline. Include who asked, what is required, and whether the request has already been answered.

Check each answer against the list above. Copilot should not invent a decision, an approver or a time that nobody posted.
