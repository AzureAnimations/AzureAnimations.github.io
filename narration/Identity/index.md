# Narrator Script — Identity and governance · Hub Tour

**Source animation:** `journeys/Identity/index.html`

**Script status:** Authored voiceover.

Nine spoken blocks — one per stop of the narrated hub tour. Stops 2 to 8 mirror the seven lessons on the learning route, so the voice and the screen say the same thing.

---

## Step 1 · Welcome

[confident] Welcome to the Identity and governance journey.
[600ms]
[calm] Seven short lessons follow one family in the Contoso house. Inside the house is identity — who lives here, who may come in, and what each person is allowed to touch. Wrapped around it is governance — which room pays for what, and the rules the house runs on. Take the route in order.

## Step 2 · Your tenant is a house

[calm] Your tenant is a house. Contoso Retail has just moved in. Everybody carries the same key, so an eight-year-old can switch on the oven.

## Step 3 · Who lives here

[calm] Who lives here. Dad, Mum, Noah and Mia have been in the house since day one — but the register holds no record of Noah, so nothing can tell him from a stranger at the door.

## Step 4 · The guest room

[calm] The guest room. Alex lives next door at Partner and already carries a key to their own house. Contoso needs Alex to look at shop-vm — without cutting a Contoso key or sharing one.

## Step 5 · The front door

[calm] The front door. Mum opens the orders app, then Azure management — and is asked to prove herself again. Worse, getting in still says nothing about what she may touch.

## Step 6 · Running the house

[calm] Running the house. Mia needs a password reset, a product license and an updated recovery phone. Give the helper only the roles needed for those tasks.

## Step 7 · Which room pays for this?

[calm] Which room pays for this? The oven, the TV, the speaker — every appliance from the first lesson is paid from the same account. One number at the end of the month, and no way to tell the kitchen from the kids.

## Step 8 · The house rules on money

[calm] The house rules on money. Mia has her own room and a door key that works. She orders a games console for it, and no rule in the house says she may not.

## Step 9 · Go deeper on Microsoft Learn

[calm] Every lesson here is grounded in official Microsoft documentation, and each stop links back to its source.
