## Step 1 · One attack, four doors

[curious] An attacker can reach Priya at Contoso through four doors. Her email, her laptop, her sign-in, or a cloud app, and each is a way in.
[600ms]
[calm] A different Defender product guards each door. We'll meet them one by one.
[600ms]
[confident] This lesson covers the threat protection skills with Microsoft Defender XDR from S C nine hundred.

## Step 2 · Defender for Office 365

[calm] Defender for Office 365 protects email and collaboration. Here it checks Priya's message.
[600ms]
[curious] It looks at the sender, the link and the attachment. Safe Links scans web addresses, and Safe Attachments opens files in a safe virtual environment before delivery.
[600ms]
[reassuring] So a risky message is caught early, before Priya ever opens it.

## Step 3 · Defender for Endpoint

[calm] Defender for Endpoint protects devices. A sensor on Priya's laptop reports its behavior to the Defender service.
[600ms]
[serious] If the laptop is compromised, you can isolate it. It's cut off from the network, but keeps its connection to the Defender service.
[600ms]
[reassuring] And once it's clean, you can release it again.

## Step 4 · Defender for Identity

[calm] Defender for Identity puts sensors on the domain controllers. They watch sign-ins as they happen.
[600ms]
[serious] Here an attacker uses a stolen account to move from one machine to the next. That's lateral movement.
[600ms]
[confident] Behavioral analytics spot it along the stages of an identity attack.

## Step 5 · Defender for Cloud Apps

[curious] People use more cloud apps than IT knows about. Defender for Cloud Apps finds the ones they really use.
[600ms]
[calm] Each discovered app gets a risk ranking, so you can see which ones deserve attention.
[600ms]
[confident] It can also limit what a user does inside a session. For example, it can allow access while monitoring it, and block downloads.

## Step 6 · Defender Vulnerability Management

[calm] Defender Vulnerability Management looks for weaknesses on the devices, and it never stops.
[600ms]
[curious] It ranks the riskiest first, using threat intelligence, how likely a breach is, and business context.
[600ms]
[confident] Then it helps IT fix them, for example with a task in Intune, or by blocking a vulnerable app.

## Step 7 · Microsoft Threat Intelligence

[calm] Behind the products sits Microsoft threat research. It explains who the attackers are and what they do.
[600ms]
[curious] That knowledge feeds the Defender products, and shows up as enriched pages for addresses, domains and files, and as reports from Microsoft researchers.
[600ms]
[reflective] Some capabilities in this area are still in preview.

## Step 8 · The Defender portal

[calm] The Microsoft Defender portal is the one console. Alerts from every product land here.
[600ms]
[impressed] They join into a single incident, so the team sees the whole attack, not four separate stories.
[600ms]
[confident] It also brings automatic attack disruption and hunting across products.
