# Narrator Script — Journey · Hand off the work with Copilot Cowork

**Source animation:** `journeys/Copilot/CoworkHandOff.html`

Tags in `[brackets]` are delivery cues — speaking styles (e.g. `[confident]`),
paralinguistics (e.g. `[laughter]`), and `[NNNms]` pause markers (e.g. `[600ms]`).
One block per animation step.

---

## Step 1 · Alex's week isn't one task

[calm] Alex leads the Beacon rollout for Northwind Traders.
[600ms]
[concerned] This week brings forty unread emails, a double-booked Thursday, a migration review to prepare, and a status report due on Friday — each one spread across several apps.
[600ms]
[thoughtful] In Copilot Chat, that's about a dozen prompts, managed one at a time.
[800ms]
[confident] So hand off the goal, not the steps. That's Copilot Cowork.

## Step 2 · Conversation, specialist or assignment?

[calm] Copilot Chat is a conversation you steer, one turn at a time. Agents are purpose-built for one scoped, repeatable job.
[600ms]
[confident] Cowork is an assignment. You hand off a goal, and it checks in when it needs you.
[600ms]
[thoughtful] Ask yourself: is it one task, a repeatable workflow, or several tasks you'd rather not manage one by one?
[800ms]
[encouraging] If your prompt reads like a long list of steps, you're describing a goal — give it to Cowork.

## Step 3 · Open Cowork

[calm] Alex opens Microsoft Copilot in the browser and switches from Chat to Cowork.
[600ms]
[confident] On the left: New task, My tasks and Automations. In the middle: a box to start a task, and workflow suggestions to get going quickly.
[600ms]
[reassuring] The model stays on Auto, and reasoning effort on Medium — Cowork picks what the task needs.

## Step 4 · Write a request that works

[thoughtful] How you ask matters. A step-by-step script is too rigid. A vague request is too thin.
[600ms]
[confident] What works is the outcome plus the deliverables: get me ready for Thursday's Beacon migration review — pull our recent threads, summarize last time, and draft a one-page briefing I can review.
[600ms]
[calm] Then add context: the people, the timeframe, the files, and the audience.
[800ms]
[encouraging] Keep the scope to one clear goal.

## Step 5 · Cowork plans and works

[calm] Cowork plans the work, then shows each step as it goes.
[600ms]
[confident] It searches Outlook for the Beacon threads, reads the rollout plan, summarizes the last review, and starts the briefing.
[600ms]
[reassuring] The side panel tracks progress, input files and the skills it's using. It draws on Work I Q — your mail, meetings, files and chats — always within your permissions.

## Step 6 · It asks when it needs you

[curious] Who should get the agenda?
[600ms]
[calm] When something is unclear, Cowork asks — pick an option, type your own answer, or skip.
[600ms]
[confident] And Alex can steer while it works. A follow-up like "add the dry-run results" is queued and picked up.
[800ms]
[encouraging] The request doesn't have to be perfect up front.

## Step 7 · Nothing sends without you

[serious] Before a sensitive action, like sending an email, Cowork stops and shows a preview.
[600ms]
[confident] Send approves this one action. More options can skip similar prompts — for this session only. Cancel stops just that action.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Always check the recipients and the content before you approve.

## Step 8 · Review the results

[pleased] Done. The briefing and the agenda email are ready.
[600ms]
[calm] Files Cowork creates land in the output folder — preview them, download them, or open them in OneDrive.
[600ms]
[confident] And every task stays in My tasks, ready to reopen.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Treat what Cowork makes as a draft, and review it before you share it.

## Step 9 · What did that cost?

[curious] What did that cost?
[600ms]
[calm] Cowork is billed by usage, in Copilot Credits. A bigger scope, more sources, more tools and a heavier model all use more.
[600ms]
[confident] Type slash cost to see what a task has used so far. Leave the model on Auto.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Your admin sets the limit, and you can request more from inside Cowork.

## Step 10 · All in one: hand off the work

[confident] Describe the goal. Let Cowork plan and work. Approve what leaves your hands. Review the results.
[600ms]
[reassuring] It sees only what you can see, asks before sensitive actions, and doesn't train on your data.
[600ms]
[calm] It can't use files on your device or delete files, and its drafts need your review.
[800ms]
[encouraging] Cowork carries out the work. You make the decisions.
