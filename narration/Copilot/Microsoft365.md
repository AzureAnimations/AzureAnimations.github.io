# Narrator Script — Journey · Microsoft 365: your first day

**Source animation:** `static/v2/Copilot/Microsoft365.html`

Tags in `[brackets]` are delivery cues — speaking styles (e.g. `[confident]`),
paralinguistics (e.g. `[laughter]`), and `[NNNms]` pause markers (e.g. `[600ms]`).
One block per animation step.

---

## Step 1 · Your first day at Contoso

[optimistic] It's Alex's first day at Contoso, and the company hands over a Microsoft 365 account.
[600ms]
[curious] So what actually arrives with it?
[800ms]
[confident] Not just Word and PowerPoint — a whole platform. Apps, email, storage, collaboration, identity and security, all in one subscription.
[600ms]
[calm] Before Copilot can help with any of this work, it's worth knowing what the work sits on.

## Step 2 · Subscription, licence, account

[calm] Nothing unlocks until three things line up.
[600ms]
[confident] Contoso buys a subscription — a plan. A seat from that plan is assigned to Alex as a licence. And a user account is created: alex at contoso dot com.
[600ms]
[serious] Miss the middle one, and the apps stay locked. The account exists, but the services won't open.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Subscription, licence, account — that's the order, and the licence is what turns a locked app into a working one.

## Step 3 · Signing in to the company tenant

[determined] Alex signs in with the work email, and lands somewhere specific.
[600ms]
[confident] That account belongs to Contoso's tenant — the company's own isolated space inside Microsoft 365.
[600ms]
[impressed] The tenant holds the users and identity, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, and the policies that govern them.
[800ms]
[calm] It's a boundary. Everything Alex does from here happens inside it.

## Step 4 · A day of work — and the data it creates

[optimistic] Now Alex actually works. Answer a customer in Outlook, join the standup in Teams.
[600ms]
[confident] Draft the proposal in Word, build the deck in PowerPoint, update the forecast in Excel.
[600ms]
[intrigued] Ordinary things — a mail, a chat, a slide. But every one of them leaves something behind.
[800ms]
[reflective] All of it becomes company work data, sitting in the tenant.

## Step 5 · Where does everything go?

[curious] So where does a day's work actually land?
[800ms]
[confident] Mail and calendar in Outlook. Chats and meetings in Teams. Alex's own files in OneDrive, and the shared team files in SharePoint.
[600ms]
[calm] Different services, one pile — emails, meetings, chats, documents, decks and files.
[600ms]
[reassuring] All of it stays inside Contoso's tenant. Remember that map, because it's exactly what Copilot will read later.

## Step 6 · How the company protects it

[serious] Company data is valuable, so administrators don't leave it open.
[600ms]
[confident] A sensitivity label classifies a file — Public, Internal or Confidential. Alex's Q four Proposal dot docx gets Confidential.
[600ms]
[concerned] Then Alex tries to share it outside, with partner at outside dot com, and the policy engine checks the request first.
[800ms]
[calm] Public is allowed, Internal gets a warning, Confidential is blocked. Labels classify; policies decide.

## Step 7 · Desktop apps or just a browser

[reassuring] The same account works two ways, and Alex doesn't have to choose once and for all.
[600ms]
[confident] There are the installed desktop apps on the laptop — and there's microsoft three sixty five dot com, in any browser, with nothing to install.
[600ms]
[impressed] Alex opens Word for the web, edits, and it saves straight back to OneDrive.
[800ms]
[calm] Same file, same account, same tenant. Only the door is different.

## Step 8 · Now meet Microsoft 365 Copilot

[reflective] After a few weeks, the tenant holds a lot: emails, meetings, chats, documents and decks.
[600ms]
[confident] Microsoft 365 Copilot lets Alex simply ask about that work. Summarise last week's emails and prepare a meeting brief — and back comes the answer, with Outlook, Teams and SharePoint listed as sources.
[600ms]
[impressed] Without it: search, read, analyse, write. With it: ask, review, use.
[800ms]
[encouraging] Microsoft 365 creates the work. Microsoft 365 Copilot helps you work with it — and that's exactly where the journey goes next.
