## Step 1 · Something happened. Where do I look?

[calm] Priya is Contoso's Microsoft 365 admin. An alert fires, a message is reported, and legal asks for files.
[600ms]
[curious] Microsoft Purview has a tool for each of those, and each one shows something different.
[600ms]
[confident] This lesson covers identifying data protection and governance risks, from exam A B nine hundred.

## Step 2 · Eight tools, three kinds of job

[calm] There are eight tools, and they fall into three kinds of job.
[600ms]
[confident] Some raise an alert or a match for you to review. Some let you explore data, activity and AI use.
[600ms]
[curious] Two let you search for content or measure compliance.

## Step 3 · DLP alerts: a policy matched

[curious] When someone's action matches a Data Loss Prevention policy, an alert is raised.
[600ms]
[confident] In the DLP Alerts dashboard you open it, read the events, judge it, and close it.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Alerts cover email, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and more, and viewing them needs a role such as Compliance Administrator or Security Administrator.

## Step 4 · Insider Risk Management: risky activity

[concerned] Insider Risk Management looks for risky user activity, such as the leak or theft of data.
[600ms]
[confident] A policy raises an alert when the pattern matches, and an analyst triages it. Reviewers can open a case for a deeper investigation.
[600ms]
[reassuring] User names are pseudonymized by default, which helps protect privacy.

## Step 5 · Communication Compliance: policy violations

[calm] Communication Compliance checks messages against your policies.
[600ms]
[confident] Those might cover harassing language, sensitive information, or regulatory rules, across email, Teams, Viva Engage, and Copilot.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Matches go to reviewers, who read them and act. Setting up policies and reviewing matches are separate duties.

## Step 6 · Activity explorer: what users did

[curious] Activity explorer shows what people did with labeled content, such as applying, changing or removing a label.
[600ms]
[confident] You can filter by user, activity, label, location or date.
[600ms]
[reflective] It isn't real time, since the data comes from the unified audit log, and it covers up to thirty days.

## Step 7 · Data Explorer: where sensitive data is

[calm] Data Explorer shows you a current snapshot of where the sensitive data sits.
[600ms]
[confident] It lists items that carry a sensitivity label, carry a retention label, or match a sensitive information type.
[600ms]
[serious] Seeing the items needs extra, restricted roles, because it lets you read the contents of files.

## Step 8 · DSPM for AI: see AI activity

[curious] Data Security Posture Management for AI shows how people use Copilot and other AI apps.
[600ms]
[confident] You see the activity, the sensitive data involved, and where oversharing risk sits.
[600ms]
[reflective] Learn now calls the newer experience D S P M, but the exam objective still uses the name D S P M for A I.

## Step 9 · eDiscovery: search files and emails

[calm] When legal asks for files, you turn to content search in Microsoft Purview eDiscovery.
[600ms]
[confident] It finds files, emails and chats across mailboxes, sites, OneDrive and Teams by keyword.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Check the statistics, preview the results, then export. The classic Content Search was retired, so it lives inside eDiscovery now.

## Step 10 · Compliance Manager: score and actions

[curious] Compliance Manager turns a regulation into an assessment with improvement actions.
[600ms]
[confident] Completing actions earns points that raise your compliance score, and each action is worth a different number of points.
[600ms]
[optimistic] So you always know what to do next, starting from a score based on the Microsoft 365 data protection baseline.

## Step 11 · Which Purview tool shows me what happened?

[curious] Pick a situation, and the matching Purview tool lights up.
[600ms]
[confident] The last line says what you would do with it.
[600ms]
[reflective] Many risks involve more than one tool. A DLP alert can lead you to Insider Risk Management, so start with the tool that matches your question.

## Step 12 · Eight tools, one question each

[calm] Eight tools, and one question each. Match the situation to the tool.
[600ms]
[confident] Then check yourself against the A B nine hundred skills measured and the official Microsoft Learn training.
[600ms]
[determined] The skills change over time, so always read the current study guide.
