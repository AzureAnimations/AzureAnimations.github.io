## Step 1 · Writing used to begin with a blank page

[reflective] Alex’s first task at Contoso was an executive proposal for the AI Customer Support Platform.
[600ms]
[concerned] Without Copilot that’s four passes — research, write, rewrite, review — and the first one starts from nothing.
[600ms]
[confident] With Copilot it’s three: prompt, refine, approve. The first pass already has words in it.
[800ms]
[calm] Copilot removes the blank page. It doesn’t remove the judgement — Alex still decides what the proposal says.

## Step 2 · The deck came out of the document Alex had already written

[curious] The steering committee needed slides, and the proposal was already finished. So why start again?
[800ms]
[concerned] The slow path opens an empty deck — create from scratch, format the slides, build the visuals, format them again.
[600ms]
[confident] Copilot in PowerPoint builds the presentation from Proposal dot docx, on Contoso’s own template.
[600ms]
[reassuring] It doesn’t invent the story, it reuses the one Alex already wrote. What’s left is editing a real deck instead of starting one.

## Step 3 · Alex asked the spreadsheet a question

[calm] Eighteen months of support tickets, across six regions.
[600ms]
[concerned] The old order is to build the model first — pivot tables, complex formulas, manual analysis — and ask the question afterwards.
[600ms]
[confident] With Copilot the question comes first, in plain English. Which region drove the September spike? EMEA — with password resets the biggest category behind the rise.
[800ms]
[reassuring] You no longer have to know the formula to reach the insight. Alex still checks the numbers.

## Step 4 · Three hundred unread, and a decision buried in them

[concerned] Alex came back to an inbox that had carried on without them — three hundred unread, one project thread somewhere in the middle of it.
[600ms]
[hesitant] Without Copilot the only route through is to read every message before answering any of them.
[600ms]
[confident] With Copilot the thread is read once, in summary, and the reply is drafted from it — using only mail Alex could already open.
[800ms]
[calm] Reading the inbox was never the job. Deciding is — and nothing sends until Alex has approved it.

## Step 5 · The meeting was worked once, not twice

[reflective] The steering committee ran for an hour, recorded and transcribed.
[600ms]
[concerned] Without Copilot that hour gets spent twice — typing notes instead of contributing, then scrubbing back through the recording for who agreed to what.
[600ms]
[impressed] With Copilot the recap, the action items and the next steps come out of the transcript as the meeting ends.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Nobody has to be the note-taker instead of a participant.

## Step 6 · This is the difference Copilot actually makes

[proud] Six apps, one project, one month at Contoso — and one assistant behind all of it.
[600ms]
[confident] Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook, Teams and Copilot Chat, as one experience across every app Alex already had open.
[600ms]
[optimistic] Microsoft 365 is where people create the work. Microsoft 365 Copilot helps them understand, create, analyse, communicate and act faster using the work that’s already there.
[800ms]
[encouraging] The value isn’t the AI. It’s what the AI does with the work you already had — and Alex is still the one deciding.
