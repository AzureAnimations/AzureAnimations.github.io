## Step 1 · Which door do I open?

[calm] Priya is Contoso's new Microsoft 365 admin. Requests are already arriving, for domains, licenses, mailboxes, sites, and channels.
[600ms]
[curious] The catch is that each one lives behind a different admin center.
[600ms]
[confident] This lesson follows Priya through the core objects skills of exam A B nine hundred. Let's learn which door to open.

## Step 2 · One tenant, four admin centers

[curious] Each Microsoft 365 service has its own admin center. Here's one tenant, with four doors.
[600ms]
[calm] The rule is simple. Find the object you manage, and you know which door to open.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Microsoft Entra and Microsoft Purview have admin centers too, but they belong to other lessons.

## Step 3 · A license unlocks features

[calm] An account alone opens nothing. The license type assigned to a person decides which services and features switch on for them.
[600ms]
[serious] What a plan includes differs by plan and changes over time, so check Microsoft Learn rather than memorizing it.
[600ms]
[curious] Copilot is an add-on license on eligible plans. It sits on top of the base license.

## Step 4 · License the group, not each person

[curious] Assigning licenses one person at a time doesn't scale. Instead, assign a license to the group, once.
[600ms]
[impressed] Every member gets it. A new member gets it automatically, and when someone leaves the group, the license goes too.
[600ms]
[calm] It works with security groups, mail-enabled groups, and Microsoft 365 groups. You need at least the License Administrator role.

## Step 5 · Domains and org settings

[calm] In the Microsoft 365 admin center, Domains connects your own domain name to the tenant.
[600ms]
[curious] Verifying a domain means proving you own it, by adding a TXT record at your domain registrar.
[600ms]
[confident] Once it's verified, people can move from the onmicrosoft address to your own. Org settings holds choices for the whole organization.

## Step 6 · Mailboxes and distribution groups

[calm] In the Exchange admin center, a mailbox is one inbox. It belongs to a person or to a resource.
[600ms]
[curious] A distribution group is one address that delivers to many mailboxes.
[600ms]
[serious] Careful, though. A distribution group only distributes email, and it can't be given permissions.

## Step 7 · Sites, libraries and folders

[calm] In SharePoint, a site is a home for a team or a project.
[600ms]
[curious] Inside it, libraries hold files, and folders organize them. Team sites and communication sites are the two common types.
[600ms]
[confident] The SharePoint admin center manages the sites. You'll find every one under Sites, then Active sites.

## Step 8 · Roles and permissions in a site

[calm] A site has three default roles: Owners, Members, and Visitors.
[600ms]
[curious] Owners have Full Control, members can Edit, and visitors can Read. Permissions flow down to libraries and folders, unless someone gives an item its own.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Manage a team site through its Microsoft 365 group or Teams team. A communication site uses the three SharePoint groups directly.

## Step 9 · Teams and channels

[calm] A team is a group of people working together, and channels split its conversations by topic.
[600ms]
[curious] Owners lead the team, and members take part. Every team is backed by a Microsoft 365 group, and uses a SharePoint site for its files.
[600ms]
[confident] Private and shared channels get their own sites. You manage teams and channels in the Teams admin center.

## Step 10 · Teams policies

[calm] Policies control what people can do in Teams. Everyone gets the global policy by default.
[600ms]
[curious] To give someone different rules, assign a custom policy. In the global policy, creating private and shared channels is on by default.
[600ms]
[serious] Policy changes can take up to twenty-four hours to apply, so be patient before you troubleshoot.

## Step 11 · Which admin center?

[excited] Your turn. Pick a task, and watch which admin center lights up.
[600ms]
[calm] The object you need to manage decides the door. Libraries and folders are managed inside each site.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Menu names change as the admin centers evolve, so confirm the current screens on Microsoft Learn.

## Step 12 · Four doors, one tenant

[calm] Know the object, and you know the admin center. That's the whole idea in one line.
[600ms]
[proud] Priya can now answer every request, because she knows which of the four doors to open.
