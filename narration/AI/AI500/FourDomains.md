## Step 1 · Blueprint

[calm] The blueprint divides AI-500 into four weighted domains.
[600ms]
[confident] Develop carries the largest range, at thirty to thirty-five percent, while the other floors cover architecture, evaluation, and security.
[600ms]

## Step 2 · Decompose

[confident] Start by breaking Contoso's claims process into distinct responsibilities rather than giving one agent every task.
[600ms]
[calm] Each specialist needs a defined scope and a level of autonomy that matches the risk of its decisions.
[600ms]
[serious] Keep the human checkpoint before money moves, because a payment decision needs stronger oversight than routine document intake.

## Step 3 · Remember

[calm] The three layers hold different kinds of state: this conversation, this team's workflow, and memory across future runs.
[600ms]
[confident] Decide what each agent can read and retain instead of treating all context as one shared bucket.
[600ms]
[serious] Separate identities and permission boundaries help contain a compromised agent, but isolation must be designed and enforced, not assumed.

## Step 4 · Orchestrate

[curious] The same team can follow different patterns, depending on how its tasks depend on each other.
[600ms]
[calm] Choose a pattern chip to compare sequential work, parallel checks, central coordination, peer collaboration, and delegated subagents.
[600ms]
[confident] Match the shape to the problem rather than choosing a pattern simply because it worked in your previous project.

## Step 5 · Connect

[confident] Tools, partner agents, and retrieved documents extend what the team can do.
[600ms]
[calm] MCP connects tools and data, A2A supports agent collaboration, and RAG brings relevant evidence into the context.
[600ms]
[serious] The recovery path matters too: bound retries, handle timeouts, and validate tool results before the workflow continues.

## Step 6 · Prove

[calm] Follow one correlation ID across the agents so a failed claim can be investigated as a whole workflow.
[600ms]
[curious] The failure gallery shows how forgotten context, drifting summaries, imprecise recall, and lost entities can change an answer.
[600ms]
[confident] Combine evaluation criteria with token budgets and loop limits to improve quality without allowing runaway work.

## Step 7 · Ship

[serious] The security floor follows identity and permission boundaries through the full workflow.
[600ms]
[calm] Check user input, tool calls, tool responses, and final output, and test how the system behaves under adversarial inputs.
[600ms]
[confident] Move through development, testing, acceptance, and production, then use a small canary release with a rollback path ready.

## Step 8 · All in one

[confident] The completed building connects architecture, implementation, evaluation, and safe operation.
[600ms]
[calm] Contoso's specialists now sit inside a designed workflow rather than a collection of unrelated agent demos.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Trace a claim through every floor and explain how each decision changes reliability, cost, or risk.
