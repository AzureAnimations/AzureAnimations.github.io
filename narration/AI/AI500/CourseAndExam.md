## Step 1 · The week

[calm] The agenda shown here spreads the course across four days, with a different theme each day.
[600ms]
[confident] Design comes first, implementation follows, and deployment and governance come before monitoring and evaluation in this delivery.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Use the labs to connect each theme to a working solution, and check your instructor's schedule because agendas can vary.

## Step 2 · Day to domain

[curious] Notice that the course days do not line up neatly with the exam's domain order.
[600ms]
[calm] Development spans more than one day, while security and evaluation cross over to different parts of the agenda.
[600ms]
[confident] Organize your revision around the skills and weights in the study guide, not only around the order of your classroom notes.

## Step 3 · Exam facts

[confident] The card separates the exam name and credential from details that need a fresh check.
[600ms]
[calm] Microsoft uses a scaled passing score of seven hundred; this is not the same as getting seventy percent of questions right.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Confirm duration, pricing, and other exam details on the official pages, including which commonly used preview features may be in scope.

## Step 4 · Exam feel

[calm] The timeline illustrates why section boundaries and review decisions deserve attention before you begin.
[600ms]
[serious] Read the instructions for your actual exam carefully, because you may not be able to return to a section once you leave it.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Use Microsoft's exam sandbox to practice navigation, rather than treating this illustration as a guarantee of the exam's exact format.

## Step 5 · Prepare

[confident] The preparation path starts with the official study guide and the agent foundations you already know.
[600ms]
[calm] Work through the labs, rebuild a scenario, and compare options against concrete requirements rather than memorizing a list of features.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Check for a published practice assessment and use it to identify weak areas, not as a substitute for understanding the system.

## Step 6 · All in one

[calm] The final card brings the illustrated course agenda, exam preparation, and study resources together.
[600ms]
[confident] Keep the four themes connected: design the team, build it, protect it, and prove that it keeps working.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Open the official links when planning your exam, and revisit the trainer briefing when preparing to teach this material.
