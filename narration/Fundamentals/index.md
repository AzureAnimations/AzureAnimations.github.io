# Narrator Script — Azure Fundamentals · Hub Tour

**Source animation:** `journeys/Fundamentals/index.html`

Fourteen spoken blocks — one per stop of the narrated hub tour. Stops 2 to 13 mirror each lesson card on the hub, so the voice and the screen say the same thing.

---

## Step 1 · Welcome

[confident] Welcome to the Azure Fundamentals journey.
[600ms]
[calm] Twelve short lessons follow one small retailer, Contoso, from the servers in its own back room to a workload running on Azure. You will cover what cloud computing is, who manages which layer, where your resources live, what drives the bill, and how you keep it all in order.

## Step 2 · Cloud Concepts

[calm] Cloud Concepts. Contoso bought enough servers for its busiest day, so most of the year that capacity sits idle.

## Step 3 · Cloud Service Models

[calm] Cloud Service Models. Contoso has not decided who is responsible for each layer of its shop.

## Step 4 · Organize Your Resources

[calm] Organize Your Resources. Contoso has a growing list of resources and no way to see how they relate.

## Step 5 · Regions and Availability Zones

[calm] Regions and Availability Zones. Contoso needs a location for its customers, application, and data.

## Step 6 · Azure Compute Options

[calm] Azure Compute Options. The shop needs web hosting, order processing and employee desktops. Contoso chooses a service for each job.

## Step 7 · Azure Networking Basics

[calm] Azure Networking Basics. The App Service frontend calls the inventory VM, which reads stock from SQL. That last connection is missing; this lesson builds the VM-to-SQL path.

## Step 8 · Azure Storage Basics

[calm] Azure Storage Basics. Contoso has product images, shared documents and order records, and they are not the same kind of data.

## Step 9 · Identity and Security Basics

[calm] Identity and Security Basics. Maya needs to inspect the shop in production, not change it. Contoso must check who she is and what she may do.

## Step 10 · Cost Management and Tags

[calm] Cost Management and Tags. The promotion is over, but the bill keeps growing. Contoso must separate the live shop from the test environment.

## Step 11 · Governance and Compliance

[calm] Governance and Compliance. Contoso agreed to tag shop resources, yet the next deployment has no Environment tag. A written standard needs an applied check.

## Step 12 · Manage and Deploy Azure

[calm] Manage and Deploy Azure. Contoso created its test and production environments separately, and they already differ.

## Step 13 · Monitoring Tools

[calm] Monitoring Tools. A customer waits too long at checkout. The shop is deployed, but Contoso needs evidence to find where time is being spent.

## Step 14 · Start your journey

[encouraging] The lessons are aligned to the AZ-900 exam objectives and each one builds on the last. Start with the first lesson, or pick any stop on the map.
