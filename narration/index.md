# Narrator Script — Home Tour

**Source page:** `index.html`

Twelve spoken blocks, in the order the tour visits them: welcome, the certification study paths (the banner at the top of the page), what the site is and how it works, then the eight team rooms (Cloud academy first, because it is the place to start), and a closing line. The site name is deliberately never spoken. Each room block names the journeys that live in that room (see `ZONES` in `lib/js/aw-office.js`); if a journey moves to another room, edit this script and regenerate the clips, because captions re-read this file at runtime but the audio does not.

## Step 1 · Welcome

[warm] Welcome to the future of learning.
[500ms]
[calm] This is an innovative, agentic learning platform, where Azure, AI and Copilot come alive as short, animated stories, each one narrated in your own language, with almost no text to read.
[400ms]
Every team has a room on this floor. Let's take a quick walk around.

## Step 2 · Certification study paths

[curious] Right at the top: are you studying for a certification?
[500ms]
[calm] Choose an Azure, AI, Microsoft 365 or GitHub study path.
Follow the lessons, review the gaps, then try a free official practice assessment where one is available.

## Step 3 · What you will find here

[calm] Here is how it works. Pick a room, choose a journey, and press play.
Each journey is a set of short animations that you can replay, slow down, or jump through.
[400ms]
Narration and captions come in English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean and Portuguese. And it is all free.

## Step 4 · Cloud academy

[curious] New to the cloud? Start here, in the Cloud academy.
[500ms]
[calm] Azure Fundamentals follows a retail company moving to the cloud, and lines up with the AZ-900 exam objectives.
When you are ready for design decisions, Azure Architect works through the AZ-305 objectives, one business problem at a time.

## Step 5 · Business users

[calm] Business users live in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams.
The Microsoft 365 Copilot journey shows how Copilot works inside each of those apps, what it can and cannot see, and where the business value comes from.

## Step 6 · Agent makers

[calm] Agent makers build low-code agents for their own workflows.
The Copilot Studio journey covers what makes an agent, how to give it knowledge and tools, connectors and MCP servers, and real examples, all from the browser.

## Step 7 · AI engineers

[calm] AI engineers work at the pro-code end of AI.
The Microsoft Foundry journey covers projects and hubs, model deployment, agents that read text, images, speech and documents, responsible AI, and how to choose your SDK.

## Step 8 · Cloud platform team

[calm] The cloud platform team runs the foundations.
Virtual machines, PaaS compute, containers, storage, databases and messaging, each explained from the first build through to monitoring.

## Step 9 · Network team

[calm] The network team connects everything.
Virtual networks, peering, firewalls, load balancing, private links and hybrid connectivity, in the order you would actually build them.

## Step 10 · Security and identity

[calm] Security and identity decide who gets in.
Key Vault, managed identities and Zero Trust, plus tenants, users, sign-in, admin roles, policy and cost, which is the groundwork every organisation lays first.

## Step 11 · DevOps team

[calm] The DevOps team plans, branches, checks and ships.
The GitHub journey follows one retail project from an issue board all the way to a deployment on Azure, with no password stored anywhere.

## Step 12 · Your turn

[warm] That is the whole floor.
[500ms]
[calm] Pick the room closest to your work, hover over it to see its journeys, and press play.
[400ms]
[warm] And if this helps you, tap the heart up here to show some love. It truly means the world to us.
Enjoy.
