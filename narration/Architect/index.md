# Narrator Script — Architect · AZ-305 hub tour

Tags in `[brackets]` are delivery directions; `[600ms]` is a pause.
One short block per hub-tour stop: a welcome, one per lesson card in display order, and a closing. Stop N plays audio/index/step-NN.mp3.

## Step 1 · Welcome

[calm] Welcome to the Architect journey: one Contoso story, with a design decision at every step.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Start with governance, follow the lessons through recovery and migration, then use the case study to bring the design together.

## Step 2 · Design governance: structure, policy and compliance

[calm] Design governance: structure, policy and compliance.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Recommend a management-group and subscription structure, a tagging strategy, and the policy and compliance tools that enforce them.

## Step 3 · Design hybrid identity and on-premises access

[calm] Design hybrid identity and on-premises access.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Sync on-premises directories, choose a sign-in method, serve legacy apps and publish on-premises resources securely.

## Step 4 · Design access: roles, PIM and access reviews

[calm] Design access: roles, PIM and access reviews.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Pick the right role system, give workloads identities instead of secrets, and keep privileged and partner access time-bound and reviewed.

## Step 5 · Design logging and monitoring

[calm] Design logging and monitoring.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Decide where signals go: workspace design, routing logs with diagnostic settings, alerts, and a SIEM on top.

## Step 6 · Design relational data storage

[calm] Design relational data storage.
[600ms]
[encouraging] SQL Database, Managed Instance or SQL Server on a VM: service tier, scaling, availability and protection.

## Step 7 · Azure Storage redundancy and protection

[calm] Azure Storage redundancy and protection.
[600ms]
[encouraging] LRS, ZRS, GRS and GZRS, plus tiers, soft delete, versioning and immutability for blobs.

## Step 8 · Design data integration and data lake

[calm] Design data integration and data lake.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Data Factory, Data Lake, Databricks, Synapse and Stream Analytics, and the hot, warm and cold paths.

## Step 9 · Design for recovery: RTO, RPO and the right tool

[calm] Design for recovery: RTO, RPO and the right tool.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Turn “how long can we be down?” into a design: Azure Backup, Azure Site Recovery, or both.

## Step 10 · Keep data available: HA and DR for databases and storage

[calm] Keep data available: HA and DR for databases and storage.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Zone redundancy, geo-replication and failover groups, multi-region Cosmos DB, and PostgreSQL high availability, matched to RTO and RPO.

## Step 11 · Pick the right compute

[calm] Pick the right compute.
[600ms]
[encouraging] VMs, containers, App Service, Functions or Batch: the questions that decide, applied to five Contoso workloads.

## Step 12 · Front the app: APIs, caching and deployment

[calm] Front the app: APIs, caching and deployment.
[600ms]
[encouraging] API Management, caching, messaging versus events, configuration, and automated deployment.

## Step 13 · Design a migration to Azure

[calm] Design a migration to Azure.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Assess what you have, pick a strategy per workload, then move servers, databases and unstructured data.

## Step 14 · Design the Contoso platform

[calm] Design the Contoso platform.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Read the brief, then make one defensible decision per domain and check the whole design against the Well-Architected pillars.

## Step 15 · Choose your next step

[calm] Choose the lesson that addresses the design question in front of you, or follow the journey in order.
[600ms]
[encouraging] Use the linked library lessons and official study material to review the wider study plan, then explain why your choices meet Contoso's requirements.
