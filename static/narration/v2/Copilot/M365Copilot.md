## Step 1 · Adding AI on top of Microsoft 365

[calm] Contoso already runs on Microsoft 365 — the identity, the mail, the meetings, the documents and the files.
[600ms]
[confident] Microsoft 365 Copilot is an add-on licence that sits on top of all of that.
[600ms]
[impressed] It isn't a separate product Alex has to go somewhere else to use. It's a layer over the platform Contoso already has.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Microsoft 365 holds Contoso's work. Microsoft 365 Copilot helps Alex work with it.

## Step 2 · Copilot Chat — open to everyone

[curious] But what if the add-on hasn't been assigned yet?
[800ms]
[confident] Any signed-in Contoso work account can open Copilot Chat and start asking straight away.
[600ms]
[calm] Alex types a question — what are the latest A I trends — and an answer comes back in plain language.
[600ms]
[reassuring] It's the free front door for a work account. The capabilities are more limited than the full experience, but the door is open.

## Step 3 · How do I know I have the licence?

[curious] So how does Alex tell whether the add-on is actually assigned?
[800ms]
[calm] Look at the chat itself. Without the licence, it's Copilot Chat and nothing more.
[600ms]
[confident] With it, the older interface showed a Work and Web toggle at the top — and the new one turns that into the Work IQ button, next to the model picker.
[600ms]
[reassuring] And the clearest signal of all: the answers start citing Contoso mail, chats and files.

## Step 4 · Work IQ on or off — where the answer comes from

[calm] That button decides where the answer comes from.
[600ms]
[confident] With Work IQ off, Alex asks what is Azure, and Copilot answers from public web knowledge.
[600ms]
[intrigued] Turn Work IQ on, ask how the Contoso Azure migration is going, and it reasons over Contoso content instead — the mail, meetings, chats and files, with sources.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Same box, same question style. Only the grounding changes.

## Step 5 · It respects what you can already see

[serious] This is the part worth getting exactly right.
[600ms]
[calm] Copilot honours existing permissions, sensitivity labels and compliance policies. It can never show Alex Contoso content Alex was never allowed to open.
[600ms]
[confident] Ask for the Contoso salary data, and if Alex has no access to that H R site, the answer is simply denied. Ask about Alex's own project documents, and the answer comes back.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Copilot doesn't widen access. It works inside the access Alex already has.

## Step 6 · Asking for it properly

[curious] So how should Alex actually ask?
[800ms]
[confident] A prompt can carry four parts — the goal, the context around it, the source to draw on, and what you expect back. Only the goal is required.
[600ms]
[determined] Alex types slash, picks Contoso Pilot Proposal dot docx from recent files, types slash again and picks the Pilot review meeting — so Copilot works from those, not from whatever it finds on its own.
[600ms]
[calm] And expect a conversation, not one perfect prompt. Read what comes back, then refine it.

## Step 7 · Choosing how Copilot thinks

[calm] Next to Work IQ sits one more control — the model picker.
[600ms]
[confident] Auto decides how long to think. Or Alex can ask for a quick response, tell it to think deeper, or reach for advanced reasoning.
[600ms]
[intrigued] There's a choice of model family too — G P T from OpenAI, or Claude from Anthropic.
[800ms]
[reassuring] Match the model to the job: quick for everyday Contoso asks, deeper thinking for the hard ones.

## Step 8 · From chat to agents

[reflective] Everything so far has been one question, one answer.
[600ms]
[confident] Agents go further. They're specialists that carry out multi-step Contoso work — gathering, analysing, and producing something Alex can hand to the team.
[600ms]
[impressed] Chat answers a question. An agent runs the steps and returns a deliverable.
[800ms]
[curious] Researcher and Analyst come built in, and there are more specialists in the agent store.

## Step 9 · Researcher and Analyst at work

[determined] Alex asks Researcher to research A I adoption for the Contoso board.
[600ms]
[confident] It pulls together Contoso files, web results and reports, and writes a synthesised brief with the sources cited.
[600ms]
[intrigued] Then Analyst gets a different kind of question — which region grew fastest last quarter — pointed at Contoso Sales F Y twenty-five dot xlsx.
[800ms]
[impressed] Back comes the answer: A P A C, up eighteen percent quarter on quarter.

## Step 10 · Keep the work — Pages and export

[reflective] A good answer in a chat is easy to lose.
[600ms]
[confident] So Alex turns it into a Page — an editable canvas that keeps the result and can be refined with Copilot later.
[600ms]
[optimistic] And when it's ready, it exports as a Word document, a PowerPoint deck, or a P D F to share with the Contoso team.
[800ms]
[reassuring] A throwaway conversation becomes a reusable piece of work.

## Step 11 · Microsoft 365 Copilot — All in One

[proud] So that's the whole picture, in one place.
[600ms]
[confident] Contoso mail, meetings, chats, documents and files — plus the web, agents and Pages — all reachable from a single prompt.
[600ms]
[impressed] Alex asks for help getting the work done, and back comes a brief drawn from the mail thread, Tuesday's meeting and the latest deck in SharePoint, ready to share as a Page.
[800ms]
[encouraging] Grounded in what Alex is allowed to see, with sources to check — and next, the journey takes that into Word.
