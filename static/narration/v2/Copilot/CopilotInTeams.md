# Narrator Script — Journey · Microsoft 365 Copilot in Teams

**Source animation:** `static/v2/Copilot/CopilotInTeams.html`

Tags in `[brackets]` are delivery cues — speaking styles (e.g. `[confident]`),
paralinguistics (e.g. `[laughter]`), and `[NNNms]` pause markers (e.g. `[600ms]`).
One block per animation step.

---

## Step 1 · Twenty-three minutes late to your own project

[concerned] The previous meeting overran, and Alex walks into the executive steering committee twenty-three minutes in.
[600ms]
[curious] It's Alex's own project being discussed — so how do you catch up without stopping the room?
[800ms]
[confident] Alex types one question to Copilot in the meeting: what did I miss?
[600ms]
[reassuring] Back comes the pilot Priya proposed, the ticket data Marco used to back it, and the one question Dana left open — and nobody else can see that answer.

## Step 2 · The meeting, written down while it happened

[calm] The committee runs fifty-two minutes, and not one person takes a note.
[600ms]
[confident] Afterwards, Copilot turns the transcript into a recap — the discussion points in the order they were actually argued.
[600ms]
[impressed] Every point carries the name of whoever raised it, so Alex can see who pushed for what.
[800ms]
[serious] Intelligent recap does need a Microsoft 365 Copilot or Teams Premium licence, and a transcript that was saved.

## Step 3 · Five actions left the room. Two of them are yours

[amused] Everybody leaves a meeting quietly certain that somebody else wrote the actions down.
[600ms]
[confident] Copilot lifts five commitments out of the discussion and puts an owner and a date on each one.
[600ms]
[determined] Two are Alex's: name the escalation owner by Wednesday, and define what success looks like at week four.
[800ms]
[calm] Copilot recommends the owner and the date it heard — Alex still confirms them before anyone is held to one.

## Step 4 · Approved: a four-week pilot, and the budget to run it

[confident] A meeting is only worth the decisions it produces, so Alex asks Copilot what was actually decided.
[600ms]
[impressed] Three things: the four-week EMEA pilot is approved, the pilot budget is released from the FY26 envelope — but not the full rollout — and Alex owns the pilot end to end.
[600ms]
[calm] Each card names the person who decided it, and the minute it happened.
[800ms]
[reflective] One thing stayed open — what success looks like at week four — which is exactly why it came back as Alex's first action.

## Step 5 · A meeting you can ask questions

[curious] The recap is a starting point, so what happens when a new question comes up two days later?
[800ms]
[confident] Alex just asks the meeting. Who suggested this? When was the budget discussed? What are the next steps?
[600ms]
[impressed] Copilot answers from this meeting's transcript and nothing else, and names the speaker and the moment each answer came from.
[600ms]
[reassuring] Click the marker and you're back at the exact point in the meeting where it was said.

## Step 6 · From twenty-three minutes late to owning the pilot

[proud] One meeting, and look at what Alex walked out with.
[600ms]
[confident] The recap, two action items, three decisions, and a transcript that still answers questions.
[600ms]
[optimistic] Without Copilot that's an hour of note-taking and a recording to trawl through afterwards.
[800ms]
[encouraging] So attend the meeting, and let Copilot take the minutes.
